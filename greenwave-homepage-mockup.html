FAQ

Close the sale before they leave.

2-prong or 3-prong — which do I buy?

Look at the wall outlet. If it has a round ground hole, choose 3-prong (Type B). If not, choose 2-prong (Type A). Both are for 100–120V systems in the US, Canada, Mexico, and Japan. European Schuko filters are a separate product.

How many filters do I need?

Use the finder above. Example: 1 bedroom house (700–1,000 sq ft) is 8 filters / $292. Kitchens and living rooms often need 3–4; bedrooms 2; bathrooms 1. A meter helps you place them.

Will this block Wi‑Fi or 5G?

No. Greenwave filters reduce dirty electricity (EMI) on building wiring. They are not RF, electric-field, or magnetic-field meters or shields. That’s the #1 confusion that causes returns.

What if I don’t know if I have a dirty electricity problem?

Start with the $200 test kit: EMI meter, two filters, and an outlet tester. If you see a drop, kit the rest of the house.
