What is dirty electricity?

Electrical Noise Can Start With the Devices You Use Every Day

Modern electronics and electrical systems can introduce high-frequency noise onto building wiring. Once present, dirty electricity can travel through circuits and into other areas of a home, office, classroom, or building.

Lighting

LED and electronically controlled lighting systems

Electronics

TVs, computers, monitors, and other electronic equipment

Device Chargers

Phone, tablet, laptop, and other power adapters

Appliances

Everyday household and workplace electrical appliances

Heating & Cooling

Modern HVAC equipment and electronic controls

Solar & EV Systems

Inverters, chargers, and related electrical technology

Once on electrical wiring, dirty electricity can spread throughout a building and even travel along power lines into other buildings.

Common questions / FAQs

What to Know Before Choosing Greenwave Filters

What is dirty electricity?

Dirty electricity is high-frequency electrical noise that travels along building wiring. It can be introduced by electronics, lighting systems, appliances, chargers, and other modern electrical technologies.

What do Greenwave Filters do?

Greenwave Filters plug into electrical outlets and reduce high-frequency electrical noise on circuit wiring.

How do I know if I have dirty electricity?

A broadband EMI meter designed for dirty electricity measurement can provide readings from your wiring, helping you compare different outlets and areas of your space.

How many Greenwave Filters do I need?

The appropriate number depends on the size and electrical characteristics of your space. Start with the Greenwave Kit Finder for a recommended setup. You can then use an EMI Meter to fine-tune filter placement using actual readings.

Should I choose 2-prong or 3-prong filters?

Choose the filter type that matches the electrical outlets where the filters will be installed. The Kit Finder can help you identify the appropriate option for your space.

Do Greenwave Filters block Wi-Fi or 5G?

No. Greenwave Filters are designed to reduce high-frequency electrical noise carried on building wiring. They are not wireless signal blockers or RF shielding products.

Do I need an electrician to install Greenwave Filters?

Greenwave Filters are designed for straightforward plug-in installation in compatible outlets. If you have unusual wiring conditions or electrical safety concerns, contact Greenwave Customer Care or a qualified electrical professional.
